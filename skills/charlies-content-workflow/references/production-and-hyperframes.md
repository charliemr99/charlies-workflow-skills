# Production and HyperFrames

Read this reference after the claim and evidence gate passes.

## Editorial Package

Derive the script from approved claim wording. Give each language a natural
hook, progression, proof, caveat, and CTA. English and Spanish are separately
timed variants; translation may seed the second script but must not force the
same sentence length or reuse obsolete timing. Natural recorded audio becomes
the edit master clock.

Create an exact shot matrix with these fields:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Start/end against the current language audio |
| Visual owner | A-roll, VO, product/site, source, terminal, benchmark, or graphic |
| Spoken line | Exact approved language line |
| On-screen text | Final readable copy, units, and labels |
| Evidence source | Claim ID and source/raw artifact ID |
| Caveat | Visible or spoken qualification and timing |
| Edit instruction | Crop, transition, emphasis, caption mode, or hold |
| Readiness | Ready, record, capture, revise, blocked, or approved |

List what already exists and what the creator still needs to record or
approve. Do not hide missing proof behind an animation.

## A-roll, VO, and Capture Guide

For each recording block specify framing, camera height, eye line, background,
lighting intent, vertical safe areas, delivery intention, alternate takes,
handles before and after each line, room tone, and expected file name. Prefer
vertical 4K/30 or 1080p/30 for social video unless the platform requires a
different format.

Preserve original recordings. Transform copies or proxies, retain take and
language identity, and ingest transcripts before final edit decisions. Capture
real product or site behavior with enough lead-in, action, result, and state to
prove continuity. Remove or mask private data before any public-safe export.

## Evidence Graphics and HyperFrames

HyperFrames is optional. Use it when deterministic reusable motion explains
evidence more clearly than real footage or a static card. Use real brand assets
and keep evidence values separate from presentation where practical.

Optimize every evidence card for mobile with large type, consistent safe
margins, compact layouts, high contrast, readable units, and restrained motion.
Before/after bars and other quantitative graphics must use proportional
comparisons and a disclosed scale; animation must not exaggerate the result.
Build deterministic timelines from the final audio duration and shot matrix.

Preview is not final proof. Validate the compiled or rendered output. If
preview and render diverge, reduce the compiled path to a minimal reproduction,
fix it, and retain a regression check before restoring complexity.

## Captions

Caption all speech with phrase- or word-aware timing. Use no more than two
readable lines. Captions may be centered over evidence, but move them toward
the lower-middle when central placement covers the presenter's face. Inspect
real frames at representative moments and transitions; coordinates alone do
not prove face avoidance or readability.

## Audio

Prioritize intelligible, consistent dialogue across both language masters.
For spoken-social output, an initial target may be approximately `-14 LUFS`
integrated with final true peak `<= -1.5 dBTP`; both remain configurable by the
delivery contract. Measure after final encode, not only on a mix preview. Do
not synthesize the creator's voice without explicit authorization.
