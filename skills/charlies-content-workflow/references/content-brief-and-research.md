# Content Brief and Research

Read this reference during the parent's discovery and spec phases. Keep its
artifacts ignored with the current run.

## Scope Selection

Choose the smallest scope that owns the requested outcome:

1. Strategy and research only: brief, sources, claims, and recommendation.
2. Evidence-backed pre-production: add scripts, shot matrix, recording guide,
   and production state.
3. Full production: add capture, edit, captions, audio, compiled masters, and
   final QA.
4. Refresh or re-version: audit existing claims and assets, identify what is
   stale, and regenerate only invalidated surfaces.

Do not force a benchmark, HyperFrames composition, bilingual variant, or final
master when the selected scope does not require it.

## Content Brief

Settle and record:

- outcome, audience, pain or desire, funnel stage, and target platform;
- target duration, content type, one core promise, and one CTA;
- requested languages and whether each needs a separate master;
- brand voice, real brand assets, visual constraints, safe areas, and required
  disclosures;
- supplied recordings, footage, screenshots, source material, prior scripts,
  and reference content;
- missing assets, creator recording obligations, approval mode, deadline, and
  publication boundary; and
- what is known, assumed, time-sensitive, deferred, or blocked.

Analyze reference content for structure, pacing, proof style, framing, and
audience fit. Reuse useful abstractions, but do not copy unsupported claims,
third-party creative, or a format that conflicts with the brand.

## Research

Use primary or authoritative current sources for factual, numerical, legal,
product, pricing, version, and availability claims. Use specialist tools only
for the portion they own: web research for current facts, video inspection for
source analysis, social-content guidance for format, and media tools for actual
production. Record uncertainty instead of filling gaps from memory.

## Source Ledger

Maintain one row per source:

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Source ID | Stable local identifier used by claims and shots |
| Source | Canonical URL, document, recording, capture, or raw log |
| Authority | Primary, official, attributable secondary, or creator-provided |
| Capture date | Date and time when the source was checked or captured |
| Supported claim | Exact proposition the source can support |
| Freshness | Recheck deadline or reason the source is stable |
| Public-safe | Whether the source and its visible details may ship |
| State | Ready, stale, blocked, or needs approval |

Do not place credentials, private URLs, unpublished recordings, or local
absolute paths in public package files. Keep private source evidence in the
ignored run workspace and expose only public-safe wording or artifacts.

## Readiness Gate

Research is ready when the scope and brief are decision-complete, each material
claim has a candidate authoritative source, freshness is explicit, missing
assets have an owner, and blocked material cannot silently enter the script.
